# Concept: Direct System
***
## Overview / Definition
%% 1-2 sentence simple definition of the concept. %%
A **direct system** in a [[category]] $\mathcal{C}$ indexed by a [[poset]] $I$ is a family of 
- Objects $A_i \in \mathcal{C}$ ,
- And morphisms $\varphi^i_j: A_i \to A_j$ for $i \leq j$,
such that morphisms can be composed in a way that is compatible with the order on $I$. 

Direct systems are used to build [[Direct Limit|direct limits (colimits)]].
